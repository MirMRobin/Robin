Research Focus

My research centers on metaheuristic optimization algorithms, with a particular emphasis on Differential Evolution (DE) variants. I investigate adaptive parameter control, hybrid operator design, and success-history based mechanisms to enhance convergence speed and solution quality on complex multimodal and real-world optimization landscapes.

ModSHADE: An Enhanced Differential Evolution Algorithm with Ensemble Mutation, Dual-Bin Crossover, and Adaptive Parameter Control
First Author · Under Review
— Under Review
Introduces ModSHADE, a modified DE algorithm built upon SHADE with a triple-mutation ensemble strategy (current-to-pbest, rand-to-pbest, and rand/1), a dual-bin crossover mechanism with two adaptive crossover rates, and a probability-based historical memory adaptation method. A safety-guided local perturbation refines elite solutions without risk of degradation. Tested on CEC 2014 and 2017 benchmarks, ModSHADE achieves significant improvements in convergence speed, solution quality, and robustness compared to standard SHADE.

Provides a comprehensive review of state-of-the-art DE variants, including TRADE, LSHADE, MPEDE, EDEV, IMODE, CJADE-M, APGSK_IMODE, and more, highlighting their performance on CEC 2017 benchmark functions.
